01 / THE SPECIES

Meet the American Red Wolf

The red wolf (Canis rufus) is one of the world's rarest wild canids. It is native to the southeastern United States and is recognized by its reddish fur, long legs, and slender body.


Red wolves play an important role in nature. As predators, they help keep ecosystems balanced by influencing the populations of other animals.

03 / THE CHALLENGE

Why Are Red Wolves Endangered?

Several challenges have caused red wolf populations to decline.

🌳

Habitat Loss

Development and changes to natural landscapes can reduce the places where red wolves live and hunt.

🚗

Vehicle Collisions

Roads can put red wolves at risk and divide their natural habitat.

🐺

Small Population

A small population makes it harder to maintain genetic diversity and recover.

04 / THE JOURNEY

A History of Survival

The red wolf's story includes decline, protection, and ongoing recovery efforts.

Before the 1900s

Red wolves lived across a large part of the southeastern United States.

20th Century

Habitat changes and human activities caused their population to decline dramatically.

1970s

A recovery program began, including efforts to protect the remaining red wolves.

Today

Conservation organizations and wildlife experts continue working to protect and recover this species.

05 / THE SOLUTION

Protecting Their Future

Conservation work gives red wolves a chance to survive and recover in the wild.

What conservation efforts do


• Protect important wildlife habitats.

• Monitor red wolf populations.

• Support responsible wildlife research.

• Educate communities about red wolves.

• Work with landowners and local communities.
